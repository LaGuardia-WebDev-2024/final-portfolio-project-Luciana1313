Music

Glam Rock, or Glitter Rock, is the best genere of music, ever.
This is because the glam rock movment not only influenced taste in music, but fashion, additude, make-up, romance, social norms, etc.
The music itself can range from hard, to soft, allowing a lot of expression within both the musicians and listeners.
Glam is the perfect mix of grit and chic.
Most famous Glam Rock musicans and bands include: David Bowie, Suzi Quatro, The Runaways, T. Rex, Sweet, Alice Cooper, The Rolling Stones, The New York Dolls, Roxy Music, etc.

Punk Rock is one of the most influentual movments in history.
The ideals weren't about one cause, but a overall genral rebellion to opression by society and/or authority, a mentality built up by anger and self-destruction.
The punk rock movment took to popularization in the late 60s and early 70s.
Some famous punk rockers include: Iggy Pop and the stooges, Courtney Love, Kathleen Hanna, The Ramones, The Clash, The Sex Pistols, Billy Idol, and Green Day.
One of the greatest things about punk is that it does not pertain to one genre, but rather is an umbrella term for describing any music sharing the same ideals.
And, despite the industrilization of 'punk fashion', the genre also has no real style, altough the staple is untented to, battered clothes, due to the orgin of the style being impoverished people.
